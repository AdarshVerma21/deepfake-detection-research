# Literature Review

**Project:** Comparative Analysis of EfficientNet-B0 and XceptionNet for Explainable and Robust Deepfake Image and Video Detection  
**Access date for web sources:** 2026-11-02

## Scope and evidence policy

This review covers image and video deepfake detection, the two proposed backbones, Grad-CAM, corruption robustness, cross-dataset generalization, and the FaceForensics++ and Celeb-DF benchmarks. A source is treated as established evidence only when its title, authors, venue, and publication record could be checked against a publisher, proceedings repository, DOI record, or official author/dataset repository. The repository has no experimental results yet; all findings below are findings reported by the cited work.

## Reviewed papers

### 1. FaceForensics++: Learning to Detect Manipulated Facial Images

- **Authors:** Andreas Rössler, Davide Cozzolino, Luisa Verdoliva, Christian Riess, Justus Thies, Matthias Nießner
- **Year / venue:** 2019, ICCV; extended journal version in IEEE TPAMI
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.1109/TPAMI.2021.3117630`; [CVF paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ICCV_2019/html/Rossler_FaceForensics_Learning_to_Detect_Manipulated_Facial_Images_ICCV_2019_paper.html)
- **Objective:** Establish a large benchmark for manipulated facial imagery and compare detection methods under different compression levels.
- **Data and architecture:** FaceForensics++ contains original videos and DeepFakes, Face2Face, FaceSwap, and NeuralTextures manipulations. The paper evaluates several CNNs, including Xception, on frames.
- **Metrics and findings:** Accuracy and AUC are reported for raw, lightly compressed, and heavily compressed data. Xception is a strong baseline, while compression materially reduces performance.
- **Limitation for this study:** The data are generated by known manipulation pipelines and in-domain performance may overestimate deployment performance. Frame-level results do not by themselves establish reliable video-level detection.

### 2. Celeb-DF: A Large-Scale Challenging Dataset for DeepFake Forensics

- **Authors:** Yuezun Li, Xin Yang, Pu Sun, Honggang Qi, Siwei Lyu
- **Year / venue:** 2020, CVPR
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.1109/CVPR42600.2020.00327`; [CVF paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Li_Celeb-DF_A_Large-Scale_Challenging_Dataset_for_DeepFake_Forensics_CVPR_2020_paper.html)
- **Objective:** Provide a more realistic and challenging high-quality face-swap benchmark.
- **Data and architecture:** Celeb-DF v2 has 5,639 celebrity deepfake videos. The paper benchmarks conventional deepfake detectors across Celeb-DF and earlier datasets.
- **Metrics and findings:** ROC-AUC and related detection measures show a substantial difficulty increase and pronounced cross-dataset performance loss.
- **Limitation for this study:** It is primarily celebrity face swapping and does not represent every manipulation family or current generative model. External evaluation must state exactly which Celeb-DF release and split are used.

### 3. DeeperForensics-1.0: A Large-Scale Dataset for Real-World Face Forgery Detection

- **Authors:** Liming Jiang, Ren Li, Wayne Wu, Chen Qian, Chen Change Loy
- **Year / venue:** 2020, CVPR
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.1109/CVPR42600.2020.00296`; [CVF paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Jiang_DeeperForensics-1.0_A_Large-Scale_Dataset_for_Real-World_Face_Forgery_Detection_CVPR_2020_paper.html)
- **Objective:** Model real-world distortions and provide a large benchmark for face forgery detection.
- **Data and architecture:** About 60,000 videos and 17.6 million frames, with controlled blur, noise, color, and compression perturbations. CNN detection baselines are evaluated.
- **Metrics and findings:** Accuracy/AUC and hidden-test evaluation demonstrate that realistic perturbations challenge detectors.
- **Limitation for this study:** Its synthesis pipeline and controlled distortions do not cover all social-media processing or unseen generators.

### 4. ForgeryNet: A Versatile Benchmark for Comprehensive Forgery Analysis

- **Authors:** Yinan He, Bei Gan, Siyu Chen, Yichun Zhou, Guojun Yin, Luchuan Song, Lu Sheng, Jing Shao, Ziwei Liu
- **Year / venue:** 2021, CVPR
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.1109/CVPR46437.2021.00434`; [CVF paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/He_ForgeryNet_A_Versatile_Benchmark_for_Comprehensive_Forgery_Analysis_CVPR_2021_paper.html)
- **Objective:** Support comprehensive image/video forgery classification, localization, and temporal analysis.
- **Data and architecture:** A heterogeneous image/video forgery benchmark with task-specific baselines.
- **Metrics and findings:** Classification, localization, and temporal metrics show the value of evaluating more than binary frame classification.
- **Limitation for this study:** Its heterogeneous task definitions complicate direct comparison with a controlled binary Xception/EfficientNet-B0 experiment.

### 5. WildDeepfake: A Challenging Real-World Dataset for Deepfake Detection

- **Authors:** Bojia Zi, Minghao Chang, Jingjing Chen, Xingjun Ma, Yu-Gang Jiang
- **Year / venue:** 2020, ACM Multimedia
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.1145/3394171.3413769`; [ACM record](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3394171.3413769)
- **Objective:** Evaluate detection under Internet-sourced, uncontrolled conditions.
- **Data and architecture:** 7,314 face sequences from 707 collected videos, with 2D/3D attention baselines.
- **Metrics and findings:** Real-world performance is substantially lower than on curated datasets, demonstrating domain shift.
- **Limitation for this study:** Unknown generation pipelines, web compression, and label noise reduce experimental control.

### 6. DeepfakeBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark of Deepfake Detection

- **Authors:** Zhiyuan Yan, Yong Zhang, Xinhang Yuan, Siwei Lyu, Baoyuan Wu
- **Year / venue:** 2023, NeurIPS 36
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.52202/075280-0201`; [NeurIPS proceedings record](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/0e735e4b4f07de483cbe250130992726-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html); [official code](https://github.com/SCLBD/DeepfakeBench)
- **Objective:** Standardize data processing, detectors, datasets, and evaluation for deepfake detection.
- **Data and architecture:** A unified image/video framework including Xception, EfficientNet-family detectors, FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF, and other datasets.
- **Metrics and findings:** Frame/video AUC, accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and log loss are supported under standardized protocols.
- **Limitation for this study:** Results depend on benchmark version, detector implementation, preprocessing, and split. This project should report its own exact protocol rather than copy benchmark numbers.

### 7. Face X-ray for More General Face Forgery Detection

- **Authors:** Lingzhi Li, Jianmin Bao, Ting Zhang, Hao Yang, Dong Chen, Fang Wen, Baining Guo
- **Year / venue:** 2020, CVPR
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.1109/CVPR42600.2020.00505`; [CVF paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Li_Face_X-ray_for_More_General_Face_Forgery_Detection_CVPR_2020_paper.html)
- **Objective:** Detect generic face-blending boundaries instead of relying on one manipulation method.
- **Data and architecture:** FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF, and other face-forgery data; a boundary-oriented representation.
- **Metrics and findings:** Cross-dataset AUC/accuracy support the value of modeling more general blending artifacts.
- **Limitation for this study:** Boundary cues may not capture fully synthetic faces or non-boundary artifacts and should not be assumed to explain backbone behavior.

### 8. Thinking in Frequency: Face Forgery Detection by Mining Frequency-aware Clues

- **Authors:** Yuyang Qian, Guojun Yin, Lu Sheng, Zixuan Chen, Jing Shao
- **Year / venue:** 2020, ECCV
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.1007/978-3-030-58610-2_6`; [Springer chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-58610-2_6)
- **Objective:** Use frequency-aware features to expose forensic clues.
- **Data and architecture:** FaceForensics++ and F3-Net frequency decomposition/local-frequency modeling.
- **Metrics and findings:** AUC/accuracy results indicate useful frequency cues, particularly for lower-quality or compressed images.
- **Limitation for this study:** Frequency artifacts can be codec- and dataset-dependent, so corruption tests must include unseen processing conditions.

### 9. Detecting Deepfakes with Self-Blended Images

- **Authors:** Kaede Shiohara, Toshihiko Yamasaki
- **Year / venue:** 2022, CVPR
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.1109/CVPR52688.2022.01816`; [CVF paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Shiohara_Detecting_Deepfakes_With_Self-Blended_Images_CVPR_2022_paper.html)
- **Objective:** Improve generalization by training on synthetic self-blended examples.
- **Data and architecture:** FF++, Celeb-DF, DFD, DFDC, DFDCP, and FFIW with CNN-based detectors.
- **Metrics and findings:** Cross-dataset AUC/accuracy improves for self-blended training on several unseen manipulation domains.
- **Limitation for this study:** Synthetic blends may not match every real forgery, and gains depend on blend generation choices.

### 10. Xception: Deep Learning with Depthwise Separable Convolutions

- **Author:** François Chollet
- **Year / venue:** 2017, CVPR
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.1109/CVPR.2017.195`; [IEEE DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR.2017.195)
- **Objective:** Introduce the Xception architecture and depthwise separable convolution design.
- **Data and architecture:** ImageNet and transfer-learning benchmarks; Xception.
- **Metrics and findings:** Classification accuracy and efficiency comparisons establish the architectural basis for the widely used deepfake baseline.
- **Limitation for this study:** It is not a forensic paper; ImageNet results do not establish robustness or generalization for deepfake detection.

### 11. EfficientNet: Rethinking Model Scaling for Convolutional Neural Networks

- **Authors:** Mingxing Tan, Quoc V. Le
- **Year / venue:** 2019, ICML
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.48550/arXiv.1905.11946` (repository DOI); [PMLR proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/tan19a.html)
- **Objective:** Improve accuracy/efficiency through compound model scaling.
- **Data and architecture:** ImageNet and transfer-learning benchmarks; EfficientNet-B0 through B7.
- **Metrics and findings:** Accuracy versus parameter/FLOP efficiency motivates B0 as a compute-conscious comparison model.
- **Limitation for this study:** The paper is not deepfake-specific. EfficientNet-B0’s forensic behavior must be measured, not inferred from ImageNet performance.

### 12. Grad-CAM: Visual Explanations from Deep Networks via Gradient-based Localization

- **Authors:** Ramprasaath R. Selvaraju, Michael Cogswell, Abhishek Das, Ramakrishna Vedantam, Devi Parikh, Dhruv Batra
- **Year / venue:** 2017, ICCV
- **DOI / official URL:** `10.1109/ICCV.2017.74`; [IEEE DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV.2017.74)
- **Objective:** Produce class-discriminative localization maps from gradients.
- **Data and architecture:** Multiple image classification/localization models and tasks.
- **Metrics and findings:** Qualitative localization and faithfulness-oriented comparisons support Grad-CAM as a practical visual explanation method.
- **Limitation for this study:** A heatmap is not proof of causal reasoning. Occlusion/deletion checks and failure cases are needed for defensible forensic interpretation.

## Cross-paper findings

1. Xception is an established deepfake baseline, but EfficientNet-B0 has stronger evidence for general image efficiency than for deepfake detection specifically.
2. Compression, blur, noise, resizing, and domain shift can substantially reduce detector performance.
3. FaceForensics++ is appropriate for controlled in-domain comparison; Celeb-DF v2 is appropriate for a difficult external test, not for training leakage.
4. Frame-based detection and video-level detection are different claims. Aggregation rules must be fixed before testing.
5. Grad-CAM is useful for attention analysis, but quantitative faithfulness checks are required.
6. No reviewed paper establishes that the exact combination proposed here is novel. The contribution must be framed as a controlled empirical comparison unless the completed experiments provide stronger evidence.

## Verification limitations

Sources were cross-checked using official CVF, IEEE, Springer, ACM, PMLR, NeurIPS, DOI, and official repository records. The official FaceForensics++ split files establish disjoint source-video identifiers, but do not by themselves establish human-identity disjointness; this distinction is carried into the methodology. An EfficientNet-specific deepfake paper surfaced in search but its complete metadata and results were not independently verified, so it is excluded from established evidence and listed as UNVERIFIED in `docs/reference_list.md`.
